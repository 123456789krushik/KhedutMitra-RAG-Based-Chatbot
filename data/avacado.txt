== AVOCADO CULTIVATION GUIDE ==

Crop Name:
Avocado (Persea americana)

Common Names:
Butter Fruit, Avocado Pear

Climate:
- Avocado grows best in tropical and subtropical climates.
- Ideal temperature: 20°C to 30°C.
- Sensitive to severe frost and extreme heat.
- Annual rainfall requirement: 1000–1500 mm.

Soil:
- Well-drained sandy loam or loamy soil.
- Soil pH: 5.5 to 7.0.
- Avoid waterlogged soils as they cause root rot.

Propagation:
- Usually propagated through grafting.
- Seed propagation is mainly used for rootstock production.

Planting:
- Recommended spacing: 6m × 6m or 8m × 8m.
- Best planting season: Monsoon or early rainy season.

Irrigation:
- Young plants require regular irrigation.
- Drip irrigation is recommended.
- Water requirement increases during flowering and fruit development.
- Avoid excessive watering.

Fertilizer Recommendations:
Young Trees (1–3 years):
- Farmyard Manure (FYM): 10–20 kg/tree/year.
- Nitrogen (N): 100–300 g/tree/year.
- Phosphorus (P): 50–150 g/tree/year.
- Potassium (K): 100–250 g/tree/year.

Mature Trees:
- FYM: 25–50 kg/tree/year.
- Nitrogen: 500–1000 g/tree/year.
- Phosphorus: 250–500 g/tree/year.
- Potassium: 500–1000 g/tree/year.

Apply fertilizers in split doses before flowering and after fruit set.

Flowering:
- Flowering generally occurs during spring.
- Cross-pollination improves fruit set.

Fruit Harvesting:
- Trees begin bearing fruits after 3–5 years (grafted plants).
- Fruits mature 6–8 months after flowering.
- Harvest when fruits reach full size and proper maturity.

Major Diseases:

1. Root Rot (Phytophthora)
Symptoms:
- Yellowing leaves.
- Wilting.
- Root decay.

Management:
- Ensure proper drainage.
- Avoid over-irrigation.
- Apply recommended fungicides.

2. Anthracnose
Symptoms:
- Black spots on fruits.
- Fruit rotting.

Management:
- Maintain orchard sanitation.
- Apply copper-based fungicides.

Major Pests:

1. Fruit Fly
Symptoms:
- Maggots inside fruits.
- Premature fruit drop.

Management:
- Use pheromone traps.
- Destroy infested fruits.

2. Scale Insects
Symptoms:
- Sap sucking.
- Reduced plant vigor.

Management:
- Apply neem oil sprays.
- Encourage natural predators.

Nutritional Benefits:
- Rich in healthy fats.
- Good source of vitamins E, K, C, and B-complex.
- High in potassium and dietary fiber.

Yield:
- Mature trees can produce 100–300 fruits per year.
- Commercial yield varies from 8–15 tonnes per hectare.

Best Practices:
- Use drip irrigation.
- Mulch around trees to conserve moisture.
- Conduct annual pruning for better canopy management.
- Monitor regularly for root rot and fruit fly infestation.
