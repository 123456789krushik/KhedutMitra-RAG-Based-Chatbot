== WHEAT CULTIVATION ==
Wheat is a cereal grain grown worldwide and is one of the most important staple crops.
Optimal growing temperature for wheat is between 15°C to 25°C.
Wheat requires well-drained loamy soil with a pH between 6.0 and 7.5.
Sowing season: Rabi crop sown in October-December, harvested in March-April.
Water requirement: Wheat needs 4-6 irrigations; critical stages are crown root initiation, tillering, jointing, flowering, and grain filling.
Common wheat varieties: HD-2967, PBW-343, GW-322, K-307.
Yield potential: 4-6 tonnes per hectare under good management.

== WHEAT FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
For wheat, apply basal dose of 60 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus, 40 kg Potassium per hectare at sowing.
Apply top-dress Nitrogen: 30 kg at first irrigation (crown root initiation stage) and 30 kg at second irrigation (tillering stage).
Total Nitrogen recommendation for wheat: 120 kg/ha. Use urea for Nitrogen, DAP for Phosphorus, MOP for Potassium.
Zinc deficiency is common in wheat; apply 25 kg zinc sulphate per hectare if soil test shows deficiency.
Avoid over-application of Nitrogen as it increases lodging risk and reduces grain quality.

== WHEAT PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Common pests in wheat: Aphids, Hessian fly, shoot fly, and termites.
Aphid control: Spray imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 125 ml/ha or thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 100 g/ha at first sign of infestation.
Shoot fly management: Seed treatment with imidacloprid 600 FS @ 2 ml/kg seed before sowing.
Termite control: Apply chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2.5 L/ha as soil drench before sowing.
Monitor wheat fields regularly from tillering to grain filling for early pest detection.

== WHEAT DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Yellow rust (stripe rust): Appears as yellow stripes on leaves. Spray propiconazole 25% EC @ 500 ml/ha.
Brown rust (leaf rust): Orange-brown pustules on leaves. Apply tebuconazole 250 EW @ 750 ml/ha.
Black rust (stem rust): Reddish-brown pustules on stems. Use resistant varieties as primary control.
Powdery mildew: White powdery coating on leaves. Spray sulphur 80% WP @ 2 kg/ha.
Loose smut: Seed-borne disease. Treat seeds with carboxin + thiram @ 2 g/kg seed.
Karnal bunt: Spray propiconazole at flag leaf and heading stages as preventive measure.

== RICE CULTIVATION ==
Rice is the staple food for more than half the world's population.
Optimal temperature: 20°C to 35°C during growing season; below 15°C causes spikelet sterility.
Rice grows best in clay or clay-loam soils with good water retention; pH 5.5 to 6.5.
Kharif crop: Sown in June-July (nursery), transplanted July-August, harvested October-November.
Water requirement: Rice needs 1200-2000 mm per season; maintain 5 cm standing water during vegetative stage.
Common varieties: Basmati 370, Pusa Basmati 1121, IR-64, Swarna, MTU-7029, BPT-5204.
Nursery management: Sow 25-30 kg seed per hectare in nursery; transplant 25-30 day old seedlings.
Transplant 2-3 seedlings per hill at 20 x 15 cm spacing for optimal plant population.
Yield: 4-7 tonnes per hectare depending on variety and management.

== RICE FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Rice fertilizer schedule: Apply 50% Nitrogen + full Phosphorus + full Potassium as basal at transplanting.
Apply 25% Nitrogen at active tillering (21-25 days after transplanting).
Apply remaining 25% Nitrogen at panicle initiation stage (45-50 days after transplanting).
Standard dose: 100-120 kg Nitrogen, 50-60 kg Phosphorus, 50 kg Potassium per hectare.
Silicon application @ 150-200 kg/ha silicon slag improves stem strength and pest resistance in rice.
Zinc deficiency (khaira disease): Apply zinc sulphate 25 kg/ha as basal or spray 0.5% zinc sulphate solution.

== RICE PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Brown plant hopper (BPH): Most damaging rice pest. Apply buprofezin 25% SC @ 1 L/ha or pymetrozine 50% WG @ 300 g/ha.
Stem borer: Yellow stem borer causes dead hearts and white ears. Apply carbofuran 3G @ 33 kg/ha or chlorantraniliprole.
Leaf folder: Rolls and feeds inside leaves. Spray chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 1.5 L/ha or lambda-cyhalothrin.
Gall midge: Causes silver shoot (onion leaf). Apply carbofuran 3G @ 33 kg/ha at 15 and 30 days after transplanting.
Rice hispa: Scrapes and mines leaves. Spray quinalphos 25% EC @ 1 L/ha.
Whorl maggot: Damages young plants. Drain field for 3-4 days and then apply insecticide.

== RICE DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Blast disease: Most serious rice disease. Spray tricyclazole 75% WP @ 500 g/ha at neck emergence. Use resistant varieties.
Sheath blight: Soil-borne disease. Apply hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1 L/ha or validamycin at tillering stage.
Bacterial leaf blight (BLB): Water-soaked lesions turning yellow. Drain field; spray copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 kg/ha.
False smut: Spray propiconazole 25% EC @ 500 ml/ha at booting stage as preventive measure.
Brown spot: Apply mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 kg/ha at disease onset. Ensure balanced nutrition.

== COTTON CULTIVATION ==
Cotton is a major commercial crop known as "white gold."
Optimal temperature: 21°C to 35°C; frost is harmful; requires long frost-free season of 180-200 days.
Soil: Deep black cotton soil (Vertisol) or sandy loam with pH 6.0-8.0; avoid waterlogged soils.
Kharif crop: Sown April-June (early sowing preferred), harvested October-February in multiple pickings.
Water: 700-1200 mm rainfall or 6-8 irrigations; critical at squaring, flowering, and boll development.
Varieties: Bt cotton hybrids (Bollgard II) are widely used. Non-Bt: Suvin, DCH-32, LRA-5166.
Spacing: 90 x 60 cm for rainfed; 120 x 60 cm for irrigated cotton.
Yield: 2-4 tonnes seed cotton per hectare; Bt hybrids give higher yield with better pest tolerance.

== COTTON FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Apply 180 kg Nitrogen, 80 kg Phosphorus, 80 kg Potassium per hectare for irrigated cotton.
Basal dose: Full Phosphorus + Potassium + 50 kg Nitrogen at sowing.
First top-dress: 65 kg Nitrogen at 30-35 days after sowing (squaring stage).
Second top-dress: 65 kg Nitrogen at 60-65 days after sowing (flowering/boll formation).
Foliar spray of 2% DAP at flowering improves boll setting and yield.
Apply boron @ 1.5 kg/ha at squaring stage to prevent boll shedding.
Magnesium deficiency: Spray magnesium sulphate 0.5% solution at 45 and 60 days after sowing.

== COTTON PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Pink bollworm: Most serious cotton pest. Use pheromone traps @ 5/ha for monitoring. Spray spinosad 45% SC @ 150 ml/ha or emamectin benzoate.
American bollworm (Helicoverpa): Install pheromone traps @ 5/ha. Spray chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 375 ml/ha.
Whitefly: Vector of cotton leaf curl virus. Spray imidacloprid 70% WG @ 30 g/ha or flonicamid 50% WG @ 150 g/ha.
Thrips: Damage seedlings. Seed treatment with imidacloprid 600 FS @ 5 ml/kg. Spray fipronil 5% SC @ 800 ml/ha.
Aphids: Spray dimethoate 30% EC @ 1 L/ha or acetamiprid 20% SP @ 150 g/ha.
Mealybug: Apply profenofos 50% EC @ 2 L/ha or release Cryptolaemus montrouzieri biocontrol agent @ 10/plant.
Red cotton bug: Spray carbaryl 50% WP @ 2 kg/ha during boll opening.

== COTTON DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Fusarium wilt: Soil-borne; use tolerant varieties. Drench soil with carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L water.
Cotton leaf curl virus (CLCuD): No cure; control whitefly vector. Use virus-tolerant varieties.
Alternaria leaf spot: Spray mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 kg/ha or copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 3 kg/ha.
Bacterial blight: Apply copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 3 kg/ha + streptomycin sulphate @ 100 ppm as spray.
Boll rot: Avoid excess humidity; spray copper fungicide at first sign of boll rot.

== MAIZE CULTIVATION ==
Maize (corn) is used for food, fodder, poultry feed, and industrial starch.
Temperature: 21°C-27°C optimal; sensitive to frost; requires warm nights for good grain filling.
Soil: Well-drained loamy soil, pH 5.8-7.0; does not tolerate waterlogging.
Season: Kharif (June-July), Rabi (November-December), and Spring (January-February) crops possible.
Water: Requires 500-800 mm; most critical at tasseling and grain filling stages; never allow moisture stress at these stages.
Varieties: Pioneer 30V92, DHM-117, Ganga-5, HQPM-1 (quality protein maize), Vivek QPM-9.
Spacing: 60 x 20 cm (75 x 20 cm for taller hybrids); plant population of 65,000-75,000 plants/ha.
Yield: 6-10 tonnes per hectare for hybrid varieties under good management.

== MAIZE FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Recommended dose for maize: 150-180 kg Nitrogen, 60-75 kg Phosphorus, 40-60 kg Potassium per hectare.
Apply full Phosphorus, full Potassium, and 1/3 Nitrogen as basal dose at sowing.
Apply 1/3 Nitrogen at knee-high stage (25-30 days after sowing).
Apply remaining 1/3 Nitrogen at tasseling stage (45-50 days after sowing).
Boron deficiency causes barren cobs; apply borax @ 10 kg/ha as basal or foliar spray of 0.2% borax.
Sulphur application @ 30-40 kg/ha improves protein quality in maize grain.

== MAIZE PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Fall armyworm (FAW): Invasive pest; most damaging at whorl stage. Spray emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 425 g/ha or chlorantraniliprole.
Stem borer: Spray chlorpyrifos 20% EC into whorl @ 1.5 L/ha or apply carbofuran granules in whorl.
Aphids on maize: Spray dimethoate 30% EC @ 1 L/ha when colonies appear.
Termites: Apply chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 4 L/ha as furrow application at sowing.
Cob borer: Spray cypermethrin 10% EC @ 750 ml/ha at silk emergence stage.

== MAIZE DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Turcicum leaf blight: Spray mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 kg/ha or propiconazole 25% EC @ 500 ml/ha at first sign.
Banded leaf and sheath blight: Apply hexaconazole 5% SC @ 1 L/ha at disease onset.
Downy mildew: Use resistant varieties; seed treatment with metalaxyl 35% WS @ 6 g/kg seed.
Common rust: Spray propiconazole 25% EC @ 500 ml/ha at early stage of infection.
Stalk rot: Ensure balanced nutrition; avoid waterlogging; plant resistant varieties.

== SOYBEAN CULTIVATION ==
Soybean is a leguminous crop rich in protein (40%) and oil (20%).
Temperature: 25°C-30°C; sensitive to frost and waterlogging.
Soil: Well-drained loamy soil, pH 6.0-7.5; fixes atmospheric Nitrogen through Rhizobium bacteria.
Kharif crop: Sown June-July, harvested October-November.
Seed inoculation: Treat seeds with Rhizobium japonicum + PSB (phosphate solubilizing bacteria) before sowing.
Varieties: JS-335, JS-9305, NRC-37, MACS-1281, Pusa 9814.
Spacing: 45 x 5 cm; plant population of 4-4.5 lakh plants per hectare.
Water: 450-700 mm; critical at flowering and pod filling stages.
Yield: 2-3 tonnes per hectare.

== SOYBEAN FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Soybean fixes its own Nitrogen; apply only 20-25 kg Nitrogen as starter dose.
Apply 80 kg Phosphorus and 40 kg Potassium per hectare as basal dose.
Apply sulphur @ 20-30 kg/ha as gypsum for improved oil content.
Foliar spray of 2% DAP at flowering and pod filling improves yield.
Molybdenum application @ 500 g/ha improves Nitrogen fixation efficiency.

== SOYBEAN PEST AND DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Girdle beetle: Makes circular grooves on stems. Spray quinalphos 25% EC @ 1 L/ha.
Stem fly: Seed treatment with imidacloprid 600 FS @ 3 ml/kg seed.
Leaf eating caterpillars: Spray chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2.5 L/ha or indoxacarb 15.8% SC @ 500 ml/ha.
Yellow mosaic virus: Spread by whitefly; use tolerant varieties; control whitefly with imidacloprid.
Bacterial pustule: Spray copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 3 kg/ha.
Charcoal rot: Avoid moisture stress; use tolerant varieties; apply balanced fertilizer.
Pod borer (Helicoverpa): Spray indoxacarb 15.8% SC @ 500 ml/ha at pod formation stage.

== GENERAL IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT ==
Drip irrigation saves 40-60% water compared to flood irrigation and improves nutrient use efficiency.
Sprinkler irrigation is suitable for wheat, maize, vegetables, and groundnut; saves 25-30% water.
Flood irrigation is traditional but has lowest efficiency (35-40%); suitable for rice and sugarcane.
Critical irrigation stages for wheat: Crown root initiation (21 DAS), tillering, jointing, flowering, grain filling.
Signs of water stress: Leaf rolling, wilting in early morning, bluish-green color in crops.
Over-irrigation causes waterlogging, root rot, nutrient leaching, and increased disease incidence.
Soil moisture monitoring using tensiometers helps schedule irrigation efficiently.
Deficit irrigation (80% of crop water requirement) is possible for wheat without significant yield loss.

== SOIL HEALTH AND MANAGEMENT ==
Conduct soil testing every 3 years to determine nutrient status and pH.
Soil pH correction: Apply lime (calcium carbonate) @ 2-4 tonnes/ha for acidic soils (pH < 6.0).
For alkaline soils (pH > 8.5): Apply gypsum @ 2-5 tonnes/ha and improve drainage.
Organic matter improves soil structure, water holding capacity, and microbial activity.
Apply farmyard manure (FYM) @ 10-15 tonnes/ha or compost @ 5-8 tonnes/ha to improve soil organic carbon.
Crop rotation (legume-cereal rotation) improves soil nitrogen and breaks pest and disease cycles.
Green manuring with dhaincha or sunhemp @ 45 days before sowing improves soil organic matter.
Minimum tillage and conservation agriculture reduce soil erosion and improve soil health.
Mulching with crop residues conserves moisture and suppresses weeds.

== INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) ==
IPM is a sustainable approach combining biological, cultural, mechanical, and chemical methods.
Step 1: Monitor fields regularly (at least twice a week) to detect pests and diseases early.
Step 2: Use economic threshold level (ETL) to decide when chemical intervention is needed.
Step 3: Prefer cultural controls first - crop rotation, resistant varieties, clean cultivation.
Step 4: Use biological controls - Trichogramma cards for stem borer, NPV for caterpillars.
Step 5: Use mechanical controls - pheromone traps, yellow sticky traps, light traps.
Step 6: Use chemical pesticides only when pest population crosses ETL; prefer selective pesticides.
Never mix pesticides and fertilizers unless specifically recommended.
Rotate pesticide groups to prevent resistance development in pests.
Observe pre-harvest interval (PHI) strictly to ensure food safety.

== GOVERNMENT SCHEMES FOR FARMERS ==
PM-KISAN: Direct income support of Rs. 6000 per year to eligible farmer families in three installments.
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Crop insurance scheme with low premium (2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi).
Kisan Credit Card (KCC): Short-term credit for crop production at subsidized interest rates.
Soil Health Card: Government provides free soil testing and recommendations every 3 years.
eNAM (National Agriculture Market): Online trading platform for agricultural commodities.
Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY): Support for organic farming clusters.
RKVY (Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana): State-level agricultural development grants.
PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana: Subsidy on micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler) systems - up to 55% subsidy.

== ORGANIC FARMING ==
Organic farming avoids synthetic chemicals and relies on natural inputs and processes.
Vermicompost application @ 3-5 tonnes/ha improves soil biology and provides balanced nutrition.
Jeevamrit: Fermented mixture of cow dung, cow urine, jaggery, gram flour, and soil; apply @ 200 L/ha.
Beejamrit: Seed treatment with cow dung, cow urine, lime, and soil to improve germination and root development.
Neem-based pesticides (neem oil 1500 ppm @ 3 ml/L or NSKE 5%) are effective against sucking pests.
Panchagavya (mixture of five cow products) applied at 3% concentration improves plant immunity.
Organic certification takes 3 years of transition period before full organic status is achieved.
National Centre of Organic Farming (NCOF) provides technical guidance and certification support.

== POST-HARVEST MANAGEMENT ==
Harvest wheat at 14% moisture content for safe storage; rice at 18-20% and dry to 12-14%.
Proper drying prevents aflatoxin (mold) development in stored grains.
Clean and treat storage bins with malathion 50% EC @ 3 ml/L before storing grain.
Hermetic storage bags (Purdue Improved Cowpea Storage - PICS bags) prevent insect damage without chemicals.
Fumigation with aluminium phosphide tablets (3 tablets per tonne) controls storage insects effectively.
Maintain temperature below 15°C and relative humidity below 70% for long-term grain storage.
Minimum Support Price (MSP) is announced by government before each season; sell at MSP or above.
Grading and sorting improve market value of agricultural produce significantly.


== POTATO CULTIVATION ==
Potato is a major tuber crop grown in cool climates.
Optimal temperature: 15°C to 20°C; temperatures above 30°C reduce tuber formation.
Soil: Well-drained sandy loam soil with pH 5.0 to 6.5 is ideal.
Season: Rabi crop sown in October-November, harvested January-March.
Seed rate: 2500-3000 kg seed tubers per hectare.
Spacing: 60 x 20 cm.
Water: Requires 500-700 mm; critical stages are tuber initiation and bulking.
Yield: 20-30 tonnes per hectare.

== POTATO FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Apply 150 kg Nitrogen, 80 kg Phosphorus, 100 kg Potassium per hectare.
Apply full Phosphorus and Potassium + half Nitrogen as basal.
Remaining Nitrogen applied at earthing up (30 days after planting).
Apply FYM @ 20 tonnes/ha before planting.

== POTATO PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Cutworms: Damage tubers; apply chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2.5 L/ha.
Aphids: Spray imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 100 ml/ha.
White grubs: Apply phorate 10G @ 25 kg/ha in soil.

== POTATO DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Late blight: Spray mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 kg/ha.
Early blight: Spray chlorothalonil @ 2 g/L.
Black scurf: Treat seed with carbendazim @ 2 g/kg.


== TOMATO CULTIVATION ==
Tomato is an important vegetable crop rich in vitamins.
Temperature: 20°C to 25°C optimal.
Soil: Well-drained sandy loam, pH 6.0-7.0.
Season: Year-round with irrigation.
Spacing: 60 x 45 cm.
Yield: 40-60 tonnes/ha.

== TOMATO FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Apply 120 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus, 60 kg Potassium per hectare.
Split Nitrogen into 3 doses (basal, flowering, fruiting stage).

== TOMATO PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Fruit borer: Spray spinosad 45% SC @ 150 ml/ha.
Whitefly: Spray imidacloprid @ 100 ml/ha.

== TOMATO DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Early blight: Spray mancozeb @ 2.5 kg/ha.
Leaf curl virus: Control whitefly vector.

== GROUNDNUT CULTIVATION ==
Groundnut is an oilseed crop grown in Kharif and Rabi seasons.
Temperature: 25°C to 30°C.
Soil: Sandy loam soil with good drainage, pH 6.0-7.5.
Spacing: 30 x 10 cm.
Yield: 2-3 tonnes/ha.

== GROUNDNUT FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATIONS ==
Apply 20 kg Nitrogen, 40 kg Phosphorus, 40 kg Potassium per hectare.
Apply gypsum @ 250 kg/ha at flowering stage.

== GROUNDNUT PEST MANAGEMENT ==
Leaf miner: Spray chlorpyrifos @ 2 L/ha.
Aphids: Spray dimethoate @ 1 L/ha.

== GROUNDNUT DISEASE MANAGEMENT ==
Tikka disease: Spray mancozeb @ 2.5 kg/ha.
Rust: Spray hexaconazole @ 1 L/ha.

== WEATHER BASED FARMING ADVISORY ==
If heavy rainfall expected:
- Avoid fertilizer application
- Ensure proper drainage

During drought:
- Use mulching
- Adopt drip irrigation

High temperature (>35°C):
- Increase irrigation frequency
- Use shade nets for vegetables

Frost conditions:
- Light irrigation at night
- Smoke generation in fields

== FARMER FAQ ==
Q: Why are my wheat leaves turning yellow?
A: It may be due to nitrogen deficiency or water stress. Apply urea and ensure proper irrigation.

Q: How to increase rice yield?
A: Use high-yielding varieties, proper spacing, balanced fertilizer, and pest control.

Q: Best irrigation method?
A: Drip irrigation is most efficient, saving 40-60% water.

Q: How to control aphids naturally?
A: Use neem oil spray @ 3 ml/L.
